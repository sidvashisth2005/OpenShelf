## OpenShelf 0.3.0: Super Admin panel

- Servers that are online now link to the OpenShelf Super Admin panel automatically. Nothing to set up.
- The Super Admin can open a server remotely through a private link that only they can use. Every remote
  sign-in is recorded in your activity log.
- The Terms and Privacy Policy now describe exactly what is reported to the panel: server name, members and a
  list of content, never your files.
- New step-by-step installation guide for admins, members and the platform owner.
